hi, diera. ♡

there's actually a lot of things i want to tell you, but sometimes i don't really know how to put them into words. so, i made this little page for you, just to remind you how special you are to me.

aku seneng bisa kenal kamu, seneng bisa punya cerita sama kamu, dan seneng karena di antara banyaknya orang yang bisa aku temui, somehow, i got to meet you. mungkin hal-hal kecil yang kita lakuin sehari-hari terlihat biasa aja, but honestly, those little moments mean a lot to me.

aku juga berharap kamu nggak pernah merasa harus jadi orang lain cuma buat bisa disayang. you can be yourself around me, even on your worst days. kamu nggak harus selalu punya cerita yang menyenangkan atau selalu terlihat kuat. i'll always appreciate the little things that make you who you are.

thank you for being here, for the conversations, for the laughs, and for all the memories we've made. i hope there will be more pictures to take, more songs to share, more places to visit, and more little moments that we can look back on someday with a smile.

i can't promise that i'll always know the right things to say or do, but i'll try my best to understand you, appreciate you, and make you feel loved in the little ways that matter. because loving someone isn't just about saying the words, it's also about listening, caring, and showing up on ordinary days.

i'm really glad it's you, diera. and if i could go back to the beginning, i'd still be happy to meet you all over again. take care of yourself, okay? i'll be cheering for you, always. ♡

with love,
- ansel
